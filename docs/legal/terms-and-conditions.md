## 8. 🔴 Offline score entry — allocation of risk

This section is important. Please read it and ensure teaching staff understand it.

1. The Platform allows Users to enter scores **without an internet connection.**
   Such entries are stored **locally on the User's own device** until synchronised.
2. **Until a score is successfully synchronised, it exists only on that device. It
   is not held by us, is not part of our backups, and cannot be recovered by us.**
3. Locally stored, unsynchronised data **will be permanently lost** if the User,
   among other things: clears browser data or cache; uses private/incognito mode;
   uninstalls or resets the browser or device; changes device; or uses software
   that clears website storage.
4. **The School is responsible for ensuring Users synchronise their entries
   promptly**, and in any event before the end of each day on which scores are
   entered.
5. **We accept no liability for loss of unsynchronised data**, which is outside our
   possession and control.
6. We will use reasonable efforts to help recover such data, but cannot guarantee
   recovery.

We give this clear, prominent warning in the [School Operations Manual](../manuals/02-school-operations-manual.md)
and in training. Once synchronised, scores are covered by our normal backup
arrangements (§10).
