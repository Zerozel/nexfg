## 4. Priority levels and response targets

| Priority | Meaning | Examples | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Critical** | No one can work, results are blocked, or data may be lost | Platform unreachable · nobody can log in · scores appear lost · paid but not activated · report cards blank | **2 hours** (standard hours)<br>**1 hour** in results week | **8 working hours** |
| **P2 — High** | One class or teacher blocked | Teacher can't see classes · class shows no students · sync failing · wrong totals | **4 hours** | **1 working day** |
| **P3 — Medium** | Inconvenient, workaround exists | Printing layout · website edits not showing · password reset | **1 working day** | **3 working days** |
| **P4 — Low** | Cosmetic or a feature request | Feature suggestions · minor display issues | **2 working days** | Considered in planning; no fix commitment |

**We set the priority** based on impact, but if you believe something is more urgent
than we have judged, say so — we will re-assess immediately.

### 🔴 Results-week escalation

During examination and results periods notified to us:

- **every** issue is treated as at least **P2**
- P1 first response: **1 hour**
- **no maintenance or releases**
- selling and onboarding stop; support comes first

## 5. Availability

| Commitment | Target |
|---|---|
| **Monthly availability** | **99.0%** (≈7 hours of downtime per month) |
| Planned maintenance | Notified **24 hours** in advance where practicable; outside school hours |
| Maintenance during results periods | **None** |

**Excluded from the calculation:** notified maintenance; failures of the School's
internet, devices, printers or power; third-party outages beyond our reasonable
control (hosting, Paystack, DNS, national connectivity); and force majeure.

### During an outage

1. We acknowledge publicly or by broadcast **within 30 minutes** of confirming it.
2. We update **at least hourly** until service is restored.
3. We tell **every** school, not just the ones who ask.
4. Within 3 working days of a P1 outage we provide a short written summary: what
   happened, the impact, and what we changed.

> We would rather tell you we don't yet know the cause than say nothing. Silence
> during an outage is the thing schools actually resent.

## 7. Remedies if we fall short

| Failure | Remedy |
|---|---|
| P1 unresolved for **3 consecutive working days** | **10%** of the current term's fee credited |
| Availability below **99%** in a month | **10%** of the term's fee credited |
| Availability below **95%** in a month | **25%** credited |
| Platform unavailable **5+ consecutive business days** (our fault) | **Full refund** of the current term, and the School may terminate immediately |
| We fail to provide a requested data export within 7 working days | **10%** credited |

**How to claim:** email `[FILL: billing email]` within **30 days** of the failure,
with the dates and what happened. Credits are applied to the next subscription
period. Total credits in a period will not exceed **50%** of the fee for that
period.

> **We will honour these without argument where the facts are clear.** Disputing a
> credit you owe costs more in a referral market than the credit itself.

## 8. What we ask of you

The SLA works both ways. To meet these targets we need the School to:

1. **Report issues through the agreed channels** — not to a personal number at midnight for a P3
2. **Include the five pieces of information** in §3
3. **Give us your examination and results dates** at the start of each term
4. **Ensure teachers synchronise** — we cannot recover what never reached us (Terms §8)
5. **Keep one named contact** as the primary liaison
6. **Respond to our questions** during an investigation
7. **Attend training** offered at onboarding

> Response targets are measured from the point we have enough information to act.
> If we ask a clarifying question, the clock pauses until you answer.

## 10. Exclusions

This SLA does not cover:

- issues caused by the School's internet, devices, browsers, printers or power
- data entered incorrectly by the School, including assessment weightings (Terms §5.1)
- **loss of unsynchronised offline data** (Terms §8)
- unauthorised access resulting from shared or compromised credentials
- features not included in the Platform (Terms §4.1)
- requests for new features
- training beyond what is reasonably offered
- third-party services outside our reasonable control

> **We will still help** with most of the above — it simply isn't covered by the
> response targets or remedies. Being unhelpful about an out-of-scope problem is a
> false economy.

---

## 11. Review

We review this SLA at the end of each academic session and may update it with
**30 days' notice**. If a change materially reduces the service level, the School
may terminate under Terms §7.3 with a pro-rata refund.
