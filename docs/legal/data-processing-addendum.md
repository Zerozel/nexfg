## 1. Why this document exists

The School enters personal data about students, parents/guardians and staff into
the Platform. Under the NDPA:

- **the School is the data controller** — it decides what data is collected and why;
- **NexaForge is the data processor** — we process it only on the School's instructions.

This Addendum records what each party must do. It is the document a school's
lawyer, proprietor or an auditor will ask for.

---

## 2. Scope and instructions

1. We process personal data **only** to provide the Platform and support as set out
   in the Terms, and only on the School's documented instructions. The Terms, this
   Addendum and the School's use of the Platform constitute those instructions.
2. **We will not** use School personal data for our own purposes, sell it, share it
   with other schools, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
3. If we believe an instruction breaches data protection law, we will tell the
   School promptly and may pause that processing.
4. We may use **aggregated, anonymised** data (from which no school or individual
   can be identified) to improve and market the Platform.

## 4. The School's obligations as controller

The School shall:

| # | Obligation |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Ensure it has a **lawful basis** under the NDPA for the personal data it enters |
| 4.2 | Give required **notices to parents, guardians and staff** (wording provided in [Privacy Policy Appendix A](./privacy-policy.md#appendix-a--notice-schools-can-give-parents)) |
| 4.3 | Obtain any consents required, particularly for children's data |
| 4.4 | Enter **only the personal data it needs** (data minimisation) |
| 4.5 | Keep personal data accurate and up to date |
| 4.6 | Give each authorised user their **own account** and never share credentials |
| 4.7 | Promptly notify us when a user should lose access |
| 4.8 | Respond to data-subject requests as controller, with our assistance |
| 4.9 | Ensure users **synchronise offline entries** — unsynchronised data is outside our control (Terms §8) |

> 🔴 **§4.2 is the obligation schools most often miss.** It is the school's duty,
> not ours, but it is in our interest that they meet it — so we provide the wording
> and raise it at onboarding.

## 6. Sub-processors

1. The School **authorises** the sub-processors listed in **Annex 2**.
2. Each is bound by a written contract imposing data-protection obligations
   substantially equivalent to this Addendum.
3. We remain **fully liable** for our sub-processors' acts and omissions.
4. We will give the School at least **30 days' notice** before adding or replacing
   a sub-processor that processes School personal data.
5. If the School reasonably objects on data-protection grounds within that notice
   period, we will work in good faith to find an alternative. If none is
   reasonably available, the School may terminate the affected service and receive
   a **pro-rata refund**.

---

## 7. Data subject requests

1. Where we receive a request directly from a student, parent, guardian or staff
   member, **we will not respond substantively** — we will refer them to the School
   and inform the School promptly.
2. We will assist the School in responding, including by:

| Request type | How we assist | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Access / portability | Provide CSV export of the relevant records | **7 working days** |
| Rectification | Correct records the School cannot correct itself | As soon as practicable |
| Erasure | Permanently erase specified records | **30 days** |
| Restriction | Restrict processing of specified records | 7 working days |

3. Assistance under this clause is provided **at no charge** up to twice per
   academic session; beyond that, at reasonable cost.
4. 🔴 **Note on deletion:** deleting in the dashboard **hides** a record (so
   academic history is preserved). **Permanent erasure must be requested from us in
   writing.** Schools must understand this distinction to answer erasure requests
   accurately.

---

## 8. Personal data breach

1. We will notify the School **without undue delay and in any event within 72
   hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting its data.
2. The notification will include, so far as known: what happened and when; the
   categories and approximate number of records and individuals affected; likely
   consequences; measures taken or proposed; and a contact point.
3. Where full information is not immediately available, we will provide it in
   phases without further undue delay.
4. We will assist the School in notifying the **NDPC** and affected individuals
   where the law requires.
5. We will document every breach and the remedial measures taken.
6. **The School must notify us immediately** if it becomes aware of a breach
   involving its accounts or credentials.

> **Definition:** a personal data breach means a breach of security leading to
> accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of,
> or access to, personal data. Unauthorised access by one school to another
> school's data would be such a breach and would be handled under this clause.

## 9. Retention, return and deletion

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Subscription active | Data retained to provide the service |
| Subscription expires | **Retained for 90 days** — the School may renew or request an export |
| After 90 days | Data may be **permanently deleted** |
| Termination | On request within 90 days we provide a **CSV export**; then delete |
| Erasure request | Specified data erased **within 30 days** |
| Backups | Deleted data persists in rolling backups for up to `[FILL: e.g. 30 days]`, then is overwritten |
| Legal retention | Where law requires (e.g. payment records), retained for the required period only |

On written request we will **certify deletion** within 30 days of completing it.

> 🔴 **We will not withhold data as leverage in a payment dispute.** An export
> requested under this clause will be provided regardless of any outstanding
> balance.

---

## 10. Audit and compliance

1. On reasonable written request (no more than **once per year**, unless a breach
   has occurred or a regulator requires it), we will provide information reasonably
   necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum.
2. This may be satisfied by a written questionnaire response, a summary of our
   security measures, or third-party certifications held by our sub-processors.
3. On-site inspection is available where a regulator specifically requires it,
   subject to **30 days' notice**, confidentiality undertakings, and reasonable
   cost.

1. Personal data may be processed outside Nigeria by the sub-processors in

3. **If the School requires Nigerian data residency, it must tell us before

## 12. Liability and term

1. Liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations in **Terms §9**,
   except that nothing limits either party's liability to a data subject or
   regulator under the NDPA.
2. This Addendum takes effect on the effective date and continues while we process
   School personal data. Clauses **8, 9 and 10** survive termination.
3. We may update this Addendum to reflect legal or operational change with **30
   days' notice**. If a change materially reduces protection, the School may
   terminate under Terms §7.3.
