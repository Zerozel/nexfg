# 08 — Operational Plan

> The company's operating rhythm is not monthly. It is **termly**, driven by the
> Nigerian academic calendar. Every process below is anchored to a week number within
> a term, because that is how schools actually experience time — and getting the
> *timing* right matters more than getting the *process* elegant.

---

## 1. The term as the operating unit

A ~14-week term. Founder workload is **wildly uneven**, which is exploitable if
planned and destructive if not.

| Week | Phase | School's state | Your priority | Founder hrs/wk |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **−2 to 0** | Pre-resumption | Planning, buying | **Renewal collection + onboarding new schools** | 55 |
| **1–2** | Resumption | Chaos, admissions | Onboarding, setup, class/subject config | 55 |
| **3–5** | Settled | Teaching | **SELLING (best window)** + product work | 45 |
| **6–8** | Mid-term / CA | First assessments | Support first score entry — **critical adoption moment** | 45 |
| **9–11** | Pre-exam | Revision | Programme delivery + selling | 45 |
| **12–13** | **EXAMS + RESULTS** | 🔴 Peak stress | **ALL HANDS SUPPORT. No selling.** | 65 |
| **14** | Closing | Report cards, PTA | **Renewal ask (peak value moment)** + collect testimonials | 55 |
| Break | Holiday | Closed | Recover, build, plan, holiday programmes | 25 |

### Three rules derived from this table

1. **Weeks 12–13 are sacred.** This is when the product either proves itself or fails
   publicly. A single school failing to print report cards on time will churn *and*
   tell the cluster. Clear the calendar; be physically reachable.
2. **Week 14 is the renewal window.** Ask immediately after report cards print, while
   the value is undeniable. Two weeks later they are on holiday and the memory has
   faded ([doc 04](./04-business-model.md) §4).
3. **Weeks 3–5 are the only good selling window** — schools are settled but not yet
   stressed. Front-load prospecting here.

## 2. Core SOPs

### SOP-1 — School onboarding (target: 6 hrs, ≤3 schools/month)

| Step | Time | Owner | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. Create school + admin account | 15m | Founder | Super-admin tool `[CODE]` |
| 2. School settings: logo, colours, address, motto | 30m | Founder | Drives website + report card branding |
| 3. Academic session + terms | 15m | Founder | `[CODE]` |
| 4. Classes + arms | 30m | Founder | Match their exact naming (JSS1A, not "Class 7") |
| 5. Subjects per class | 45m | Founder | Their curriculum, their names |
| 6. **Student import** | 90m | **Founder — do not delegate to the school** | The #1 abandonment point |
| 7. Staff accounts + class/subject assignment | 45m | Founder | `[CODE]` |
| 8. Assessment structure (CA1, CA2, Exam + weights) | 30m | Founder | Must match their existing scheme exactly |
| 9. Publish public website | 20m | Founder | **The emotional win — do it while they watch** |
| 10. Train admin | 60m | Founder | Screen-record it; becomes reusable training material |
| 11. Train 2–3 teachers on score entry | 45m | Founder | **Include the offline demo** |
| 12. Print one real report card, hand it over | 20m | Founder | Physical proof |

**Go-live criterion:** one teacher has entered real scores for one real class and one
report card has been printed. Anything less is not onboarded — it is registered.

### SOP-2 — Results-week support

**Prepare (week 11):**
- WhatsApp every school: *"Exams start soon. Reply with any teacher who hasn't entered scores yet."*
- Check each school's score-completeness; chase the gaps *before* they become emergencies
- Verify subscription status — `expired` blocks access `[CODE]` and week 12 is the worst possible time to discover that

**During (weeks 12–13):**
- Respond within 2 hours, 8am–8pm
- Be physically available to visit any school within 3 hours
- Watch for: sync failures, missing assessments, wrong weightings, print layout issues

**After (week 14):**
- Confirm every school printed successfully
- Collect one testimonial per school
- **Make the renewal ask**

### SOP-3 — Renewal (weeks 13–14, ~3 weeks total)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| W13 D1 | Send a **term value summary**: students managed, report cards printed, hours saved |
| W13 D3 | WhatsApp the proprietor: *"Next term's invoice — want to pay per term or per session?"* |
| W14 D1 | Send the payment link `[CODE]` |
| W14 D3 | Follow up (phone, not text) |
| W14 D5 | Visit in person if unpaid |
| Break W1 | Final follow-up + churn diagnosis if lost |

**Every lost school gets a written 3-line post-mortem.** At 15 schools you cannot
afford to lose one to a cause you failed to record.

### SOP-4 — Programme delivery (from Term 2)

| Step | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm school + dates (before term starts) | Programme Coordinator / Founder | Schools plan the term in advance |
| Send parent offer letter + payment link | Founder | Via the school, in their name |
| Close enrolment at week 2 | Founder | Fixed cohort enables planning |
| Assign facilitator | Coordinator | **Safeguarding check first — non-negotiable** |
| Deliver 6 sessions (weeks 3–10) | Facilitator | 90 min each |
| Collect attendance + outputs | Facilitator | **This is your sponsor evidence** |
| Showcase to parents (week 11) | Coordinator | Drives next term's take-up |
| Issue certificates | Founder | QR-verifiable → moat L4 |

### SOP-5 — Support (steady state)

| Channel | SLA | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp (primary) | 4 hrs, 8am–8pm | **This is how Nigerian schools communicate. Do not fight it.** |
| Phone | Same day | For panic situations |
| On-site | Within 48 hrs (results week: 3 hrs) | Only when remote fails |
| Email | 24 hrs | Formal matters, invoices |

**Standing rule: log every support request in one sheet.** Any question asked three
times becomes either a product fix or a training video. This is the mechanism that
makes founder-hours-per-school fall over time ([doc 05](./05-financial-model.md) §8
metric 6) — without it, you scale linearly and stall.

## 3. Annual calendar

| Month | Operational focus | Commercial focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Aug** | Platform work, prep for T1 | **Paid holiday programme** (fills the ₦0 month) |
| **Sept** | Onboard T1 cohort; T1 renewals | Peak sales window |
| Oct | Support, CA cycle | Selling weeks 3–5 |
| Nov | Programme delivery | Prospecting for T2 |
| **Dec** | 🔴 Results week + renewals | Testimonials, referrals |
| **Jan** | Onboard T2 cohort; T2 renewals | Second peak sales window |
| Feb | Support, programmes | Selling |
| Mar | Programme showcase | Sponsor conversations |
| **Apr** | 🔴 Results + renewals; T3 starts | Renewals |
| May | Programmes | NAPPS presentations |
| Jun | **Inter-school competition** | Maximum PR value |
| **Jul** | 🔴 Results + session-end | Session renewals, promotion processing |

**Two structural notes:** (1) three 🔴 peak months per year — never schedule anything
else in December, April or July. (2) The competition belongs in **June**, when the
academic pressure is lowest and the PR value carries into September's sales window.

## 4. Quality gates

Never break these, regardless of commercial pressure.

| Gate | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding cap** | ≤3 schools/month | Beyond this, adoption fails and they churn |
| **Go-live** | Real scores entered + 1 report card printed | Otherwise not onboarded |
| **Data safety** | Daily automated backup verified weekly | [doc 02](./02-product-claims-audit.md) H5 |
| **Results week** | Zero new onboarding, zero selling | Existing schools outrank new ones |
| **Safeguarding** | No facilitator with students without a check + a school staff member present | Non-negotiable, always |
| **Feature promises** | Nothing promised that is not shipped | [doc 02](./02-product-claims-audit.md) |
| **Discounting** | No discount below published pricing | Protects thin margin, avoids cluster price leakage |

## 6. Continuity — the solo-founder single point of failure `[RISK]`

You are one person holding every credential, relationship and piece of knowledge. If
you are ill for two weeks during results week, **schools lose their term.** Cheap
insurance:

| Risk | Mitigation | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Founder unavailable | **Written runbook** for the 5 critical operations (create school, reset password, run sync, print batch, check subscription) | 4 hrs |
| Credentials lost | Password manager + sealed offline recovery copy with one trusted person | ₦0 |
| Data loss | Automated daily backups + monthly **restore test** (an untested backup is not a backup) | ₦8k/mo |
| Knowledge loss | This `business/` folder + recorded training videos | Done |
| Health/burnout | Enforce the holiday-week 25 hrs; the term rhythm has natural recovery windows — use them | ₦0 |

## 7. When to hire — trigger-based, never calendar-based

| Trigger | Hire | Why then |
|---|---|---|
| Support >15 hrs/week for 3 consecutive weeks | Part-time support assistant | Support is displacing selling |
| ≥5 schools want programmes | Programme Coordinator | Delivery becomes the bottleneck |
| Founder coding <10 hrs/week for a full term | Part-time engineer | Product is stagnating |
| Selling >20 hrs/week and pipeline still starved | Sales/onboarding rep | Growth is capacity-limited |
| ≥20 schools | Bookkeeper (part-time) | Compliance and reconciliation load |

Full role definitions, comp bands and no-budget hiring mechanisms:
[doc 10](./10-roles-and-hiring-plan.md).

## 8. Operational metrics — five weekly numbers

| # | Metric | Target | Signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Schools live / paid this term | ≥90% paid | Revenue integrity |
| 2 | **Active teachers entering scores / total teachers** | ≥70% | 🔴 **The best leading indicator of churn** |
| 3 | Support requests per school per week | <2 | Product friction |
| 4 | Founder hrs per school per week | Falling | Scalability |
| 5 | Report cards printed per school per term | ≥1 per student | Core value delivered |

**Metric 2 is the one to watch above all others.** A school whose teachers have
stopped entering scores has already churned — it just hasn't told you yet. Intervene
the week it drops below 50%, not at renewal time when it is too late.
